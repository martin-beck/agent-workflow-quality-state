# AR-0043: Evidence lineage publication and retention lifecycle

## Outcome

AWQ represents immutable observation lineage, truthful publication outcomes and deterministic retention candidates without authorizing destructive hosting operations.

## Scope

- Define append-only observation and decision records with parent digest, source revision, verifier-contract digest, artifact digest, evidence class, attempt identity and outcome distinct from score.
- Define publication states for required and optional artifacts, prerequisites, cancellation and explicit unavailability. Required quality gates may never be hidden by `continue-on-error`.
- Extend retention inventories with artifact role, size, time, source head, run state and provenance completeness.
- Produce deterministic dry-run candidates using protected references, minimum age, diagnostic windows, high/low watermark hysteresis and a maximum selection count.
- Protect active runs, current main, open PR heads, releases, recent diagnostics and unknown provenance.

## Acceptance

- Hash-chain fixtures reject mutation, reordering, broken parents, wrong source revisions and reused attempt identities.
- Publication fixtures distinguish successful validation from failed or unavailable upload.
- Retention fixtures prove stable ordering, bounded selection, protected-reference safety and non-authorizing output.
- Evidence remains metadata-only and contains no hosting credentials, URLs with private identifiers or artifact contents.
- Reliability documentation and generated contracts state that AWQ never performs deletion or upload.

## Non-goals

GitHub artifact deletion, storage billing, external publication and legal retention decisions remain host and consumer responsibilities.
