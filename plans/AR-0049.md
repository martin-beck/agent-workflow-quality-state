# AR-0049: Statistical experiment evidence profile

## Outcome

AWQ validates that measured quality and reliability claims disclose a predeclared, reproducible experiment design and its uncertainty without imposing benchmark-specific scoring.

## Scope

- Define an experiment receipt with source/workload/config digests, population, samples, repeats, warmup, precision target, confidence method, stopping rule and resource budget.
- Record open/closed load, concurrency, cache state, network state, retries, timeouts, cancellations, contamination and missing observations.
- Require explicit treatment of coordinated omission, survivorship bias and highest-tested versus inferred capacity where applicable.
- Bind raw-result artifacts by digest while retaining only bounded aggregate metadata in AWQ evidence.
- Separate mechanical validation of the receipt from environmental validity and human methodology review.

## Acceptance

- Fixtures cover deterministic small experiments and consumer-owned performance studies.
- Negative fixtures reject post-hoc sample changes, omitted failures, impossible confidence claims, unreported contamination, mixed revisions, unbounded stopping and upward capacity inference.
- Repeated evaluation of the same receipt is deterministic and offline.
- AWQ does not automatically update baselines or accept regressions because of variance.
- Documentation clearly limits what a valid receipt proves.

## Non-goals

ASB scoring, SLOs, workload selection, capacity thresholds, quantile estimators and domain-specific performance conclusions remain downstream.
