# AR-0030: Terminology registry and enforcement

## Outcome

AWQ provides a versioned machine-readable terminology registry and deterministic gate for canonical vocabulary, forbidden synonyms, scopes, and exceptions.

## Acceptance

- A strict schema supports identifiers, canonical labels, aliases, scope, severity, case policy, and bounded exceptions.
- Markdown, JSON, and selected text formats are checked deterministically with minimized findings.
- Positive, forbidden-synonym, scope, fixture, Unicode, and generated-content fixtures exist.
- The gate is opt-in, offline, bounded, privacy-safe, and explicitly lexical rather than semantic proof.
