# AR-0003: Policy governance and exception lifecycle

## Outcome

Ensure quality policy can only be weakened through explicit, owned, expiring and review-enforced changes.

## Scope and decomposition

- Compare all semantic policy fields, thresholds, scopes, commands, pins and evidence classes.
- Validate exception owner, approval reference, maximum lifetime, renewal history and compensating evidence.
- Generate CODEOWNERS suggestions and verify public hosting rulesets through a separately classified online gate.
- Define emergency override and revocation records without embedding credentials.

## Acceptance

- Mutation tests prove every weakening dimension is detected.
- Expired, broadened, orphaned and silently renewed exceptions fail.
- Hosting observations are time-stamped environmental evidence and never presented as offline proof.
