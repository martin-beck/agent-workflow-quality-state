# AR-0032: Native-gate and requirement mapping contracts

## Outcome

Consumers can bind AWQ requirements to existing native commands and report equivalence, coverage, and limitations without replacing project gates.

## Acceptance

- A versioned mapping schema identifies requirement, native command, tool pin, tier, evidence class, input scope, and limitation.
- Mappings reject missing or contradictory evidence and remain deterministic and privacy-safe.
- Adapter and native results remain distinguishable; no mapping overclaims certification.
- Coordinator-style Python, schema, documentation, formal, and coverage examples are covered.
