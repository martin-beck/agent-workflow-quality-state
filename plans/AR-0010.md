# AR-0010: Performance, flake and evidence-retention budgets

## Outcome

Keep AWQ fast, deterministic and privacy-bounded as repositories and check families grow.

## Scope and decomposition

- Benchmark repository discovery, checks, evidence and policy diff by tracked-file count and size.
- Detect nondeterministic output and flaky local extensions through repeat trials.
- Define evidence count, size, age and redaction budgets plus cleanup guidance.
- Split performance, determinism and retention into child ARs when baselines exist.

## Acceptance

- Regression thresholds use reproducible fixtures and statistical limitations are documented.
- Timeouts and truncation are visible failures, not silent passes.
- Retained evidence is sufficient for audit without retaining source content.
