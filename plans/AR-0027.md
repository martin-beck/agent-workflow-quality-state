# AR-0027: Formal implementation refinement contracts

## Outcome

A strict, privacy-preserving contract connects bounded model evidence to implementation traces with explicit obligations and non-claims.

## Acceptance

- Schema and evaluator require complete versioned state/action/refinement mappings and assumptions.
- Positive, missing-map, contradictory-trace and out-of-bound fixtures are tested offline.
- Reports never claim refinement when obligations are incomplete or execution is caller-declared.
