# AR-0057: Exact repository-security revision ranges

## Outcome

Repository-security adapters execute introduced-history scans against caller-supplied exact
40-hex revisions with bounded, privacy-safe behavior and complete positive/negative coverage.

## Scope

- Replace catalog placeholders with a validated argument-array mechanism that substitutes exact
  base/head revisions and rejects malformed, ambiguous, or shallow-history ranges.
- Preserve offline execution, bounded output/timeouts, descendant cleanup, hostile-diagnostic
  sanitization, and no secret/source retention.
- Add tests for clean/defective introduced ranges, later-removed secrets, absent/skewed tools,
  checksums, unsupported architecture, invalid/ambiguous revisions, shallow history, timeouts,
  oversized output, descendants, and hostile diagnostics.

## Acceptance

- Focused and full applicable gates pass, including generated catalog, contracts, SBOM/provenance,
  Ruff, mypy, privacy, determinism, and exact-head hosted Verify/Portable workflows.
- Exact revision inputs are validated and visibly present in executed argv; no literal placeholders
  reach gitleaks.
- Tests prove secret values never appear in findings/evidence and all bounded-failure paths work.
- Native/device equivalence is optional, non-authorizing evidence and never blocks hosted/container/
  VM acceptance.
- Signed conventional DCO commit, independent exact-head review, clean PR, green CI, merge, and
  post-merge verification are durably recorded. No release is published by this AR.

## Safeguards

Do not weaken catalog immutability, native coordinator gates, terminology contracts, offline
boundaries, or evidence privacy. Do not add runtime dependencies, network downloads, telemetry,
credentials, private paths, prompts, transcripts, or unbounded evidence.
