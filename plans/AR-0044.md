# AR-0044: Cross-ecosystem vulnerability and workspace supply policy

## Outcome

AWQ evaluates time-dependent advisory observations and proves that every declared executable dependency workspace has locked, scoped supply-chain evidence.

## Scope

- Define vulnerability observations with ecosystem, exact package/version, aliases, dependency scope, reachability, lock digest, scanner/database identity, observation time and expiry.
- Define exact-coordinate, expiring exceptions with owner, rationale, review and compensating evidence; prohibit wildcard or non-expiring acceptance.
- Prevent test-only exceptions from applying to production-reachable coordinates.
- Extend supply policy to a bounded list of named manifests, lockfiles and policy files, including independent Rust fuzz or tool workspaces.
- Preserve immutable advisory snapshots and separate offline reproducibility from current advisory freshness.

## Acceptance

- Fixtures cover Rust and JVM graphs plus an auxiliary fuzz workspace.
- Negative fixtures reject broad exceptions, expired observations, version mismatch, unlocked graphs, omitted executed workspaces, production reachability under test exceptions and mutable advisory inputs.
- Findings are coordinate- and digest-based without dependency source excerpts.
- Existing Rust and Android/JVM supply adapters remain compatible and retain locked/offline behavior.
- Scheduled environmental evidence is never described as a timeless proof of safety.

## Non-goals

AWQ does not contact advisory services during runtime, determine exploitability or replace ecosystem-native dependency resolution.
