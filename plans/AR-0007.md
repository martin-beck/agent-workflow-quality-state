# AR-0007: Reproducible releases, SBOM and provenance

## Outcome

Let consumers verify what source, dependencies, policy registry and workflow produced each AWQ artifact.

## Scope and decomposition

- Produce SPDX SBOMs, checksums, signed tags and SLSA-compatible provenance.
- Compare independently built wheels and source archives under a documented environment.
- Define an offline-verifiable release manifest and explicit trust-root rotation.
- Split artifact reproducibility, SBOM and attestation work into child ARs.

## Acceptance

- Fresh builds match documented reproducibility expectations or record bounded differences.
- Consumer update verifies digest and signature before changing its lock.
- Release assets contain no machine paths, credentials or mutable dependency references.
