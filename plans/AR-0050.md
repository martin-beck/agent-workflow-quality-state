# AR-0050: Language-neutral structural refactoring verification

## Outcome

AWQ validates bounded structural transformation plans and post-mutation convergence across languages while leaving mutation and semantic ownership downstream.

## Scope

- Define a closed recipe with tool/version/digest, parser/language, include/exclude scope, prohibited classes, risk, behavior claim, preconditions, invariants and budgets.
- Require positive, negative and golden fixtures plus focused and full verification argv.
- Bind plans to exact base commit, catalog, recipe, input and proposed-output digests.
- Verify unchanged base/catalog identity, budget adherence, exact output digest and convergence on a second planning pass.
- Restrict any future autonomous application to separately reviewed low-risk allowlists; initial implementation is read-only plan and post-result verification.

## Acceptance

- Fixtures cover at least two language-neutral structural cases and one mapping to the existing Python refactoring family.
- Negative fixtures reject command strings, unsafe scope, prohibited transformations, stale base, catalog drift, line/file/match budget excess, output mismatch and non-convergence.
- Tool execution, if added, uses a pinned offline adapter with exact argv and discarded output.
- Evidence contains paths only where repository-relative and necessary; source excerpts and diffs are excluded.
- Consumer/coordinator mutation remains outside AWQ.

## Non-goals

AWQ does not approximate AST semantics with regex, choose refactors, modify product files or claim behavioral equivalence without consumer evidence.
