# AR-0026: Formal policy lifecycle and concurrency models

## Outcome

Bounded executable models cover policy and evidence lifecycle transitions that AR-0006 deliberately leaves outside its review/promotion core.

## Acceptance

- Machine-readable state, bounds, assumptions and refinement limitations are versioned.
- Exhaustive bounded baseline and deterministic stale/expiry/tier/rollback/concurrency/crash counterexamples run offline and fail closed when incomplete.
- Tests cover atomicity, recovery and privacy boundaries; no hosted or native gate is removed.
