# AR-0037: Capability maturity and claim registry

## Outcome

Product capability claims become explicit, evidence-bound and resistant to inflation from planned code, synthetic fixtures or stale observations.

## Scope

- Add a closed capability registry with stable ID, maturity, source scope, supported surfaces, owner, limitations, review provenance and evidence references.
- Define reviewed maturity states including `planned`, `foundation`, `implemented`, `integrated`, `environment-verified`, `unsupported` and `deprecated`.
- Bind evidence through AR-0034 identities with exact source revision, class, digest, observation time and freshness.
- Enforce cross-field transition rules: exposed integration needs a surface, environmental verification needs environmental evidence, and unsupported or deprecated states need rationale.
- Generate claim and limitation documentation suitable for external review.

## Acceptance

- Positive fixtures cover incremental maturity and explicit non-claims.
- Hostile fixtures reject synthetic-as-live promotion, missing source paths, contradictory surfaces, duplicate IDs, unreviewed maturity jumps, stale evidence and evidence from another revision.
- Status changes are visible in policy-diff and cannot be hidden in prose.
- Registry evaluation remains offline and content-minimized.
- Consumer adoption can preserve richer downstream terminology through explicit mappings rather than weakening the AWQ vocabulary.

## Non-goals

AWQ does not determine product roadmap priority, usability, market support or operational readiness without consumer-supplied evidence.
