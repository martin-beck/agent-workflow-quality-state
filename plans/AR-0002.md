# AR-0002: Standards traceability and control catalogue

## Outcome

Version and generate reviewable mappings to NIST SSDF, SLSA, SPDX, OpenSSF and relevant language standards while explicitly distinguishing alignment from certification.

## Scope and decomposition

- Pin source editions, source URLs and control identifiers; record mapping rationale and reviewer.
- Add schemas for control sources and many-to-many requirement mappings.
- Generate per-profile matrices, coverage gaps and source-version drift reports.
- Split source families into child ARs when independent review is useful.

## Acceptance

- Unknown controls and floating source editions fail validation.
- Every mapping has rationale, evidence class and limitation; generated documents are deterministic.
- Tests prove stale, removed and falsely certified mappings fail.
