# AR-0040: Generic structured test-report evidence

## Outcome

AWQ provides a reusable, language-neutral JUnit evidence primitive that proves declared suites actually executed and did not become false-green through missing or empty reports.

## Scope

- Define a closed observation schema binding source, producer, exact declared report set, report digests, modules/suites, discovered and executed counts, skips, failures, errors and freshness.
- Parse JUnit XML with strict file/count/byte/depth limits and with DTDs and external entities prohibited.
- Require reports to remain beneath declared repository-relative roots and reject symlink traversal, unexpected reports and missing modules.
- Support explicit minimum counts and skip policies without imposing one universal testing framework.
- Refactor Android/JVM connected-test evidence to consume the shared primitive without weakening device-specific fields.

## Acceptance

- Fixtures cover Python, JVM host and mixed-module JUnit producers.
- Hostile fixtures reject malformed XML, entities, negative or inconsistent counts, empty suites, all-skipped suites, failures, missing/orphaned reports, stale/wrong-revision reports and unsafe paths.
- Artifact upload availability is never treated as test-execution evidence.
- Result evidence contains counts, classifications and digests only.
- Existing Android/JVM acceptance and native-equivalence tests remain green.

## Non-goals

Coverage XML and SARIF are future versioned extensions; AWQ does not invoke the underlying test runner in this evaluator.
