# AR-0046: Rust critical coverage and auxiliary-workspace assurance

## Outcome

Rust assurance prevents workspace aggregates from masking weak critical crates and prevents independently executed Cargo workspaces from escaping supply review.

## Scope

- Extend coverage policy with an optional workspace floor and a sorted package-to-floor map.
- Classify named packages as required, deferred or absent with reviewed rationale and separate denominator/result digests.
- Require every declared fuzz, mutation, tooling or other executed Cargo workspace to provide locked metadata and source, license and advisory policy.
- Keep exact stable toolchains, offline Cargo homes, external target/scratch directories and output minimization.
- Generate an explicit coverage and workspace inventory for review.

## Acceptance

- Fixtures prove one well-covered package cannot compensate for a critical package below its floor.
- Negative cases cover missing packages, denominator drift, duplicate names, excluded uncovered code, unreviewed deferred status and absent auxiliary locks or policies.
- Fuzz and mutation workspaces are checked before execution against immutable advisory inputs.
- Results expose only package identifiers, counts, percentages and digests, not private source paths or diagnostics.
- Existing Rust coverage, fuzz, mutation, supply and native-equivalence gates remain green.

## Non-goals

AWQ does not prescribe universal percentage values, crate architecture or benchmark-specific test adequacy.
