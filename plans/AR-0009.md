# AR-0009: Property, fuzz, mutation and adversarial testing

## Outcome

Continuously challenge parser confinement, fail-closed behavior, privacy redaction and gate sensitivity with reproducible generated cases.

## Scope and decomposition

- Add deterministic property generators for policy, schema, path and workflow inputs.
- Add mutation scores for critical check dispatch and weakening detection.
- Fuzz bounded parsers and evidence serialization with retained minimal reproducers.
- Separate fast PR properties from scheduled resource-intensive campaigns.

## Acceptance

- Seeds and bounds are recorded; failures minimize to public-safe fixtures.
- Critical mutations have explicit score thresholds and justified survivors.
- No fuzz artifact leaks inspected content or machine-private paths.
