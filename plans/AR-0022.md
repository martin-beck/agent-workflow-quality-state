# AR-0022: Bounded Rust coverage fuzz and mutation evidence

## Outcome

Provide opt-in coverage, dated-nightly fuzz and mutation contracts whose inputs, operators,
resources and conclusions are explicitly bounded.

## Scope

- Pin cargo-llvm-cov, cargo-fuzz, cargo-mutants and the required stable/dated-nightly components with
  checksum-verified bounded atomic Linux x86-64 acquisition.
- Define separate coverage, fixed-run fuzz regression and mutation-sentinel contracts; keep open-ended
  campaigns outside ordinary local/PR execution.
- Require repository-owned target lists, seed corpora, mutation filters and coverage floors.
- Place corpora, artifacts, target directories and mutation output outside the source tree; require
  clean tracked state and lock integrity after execution.
- Add controlled positive and negative fixtures for threshold failure, crashing seed, retained
  mutant, missing target/component, timeout and resource-bound behavior.
- Emit content-minimized summaries that identify declared bounds without compiler diagnostics,
  source excerpts, corpus contents or private paths.

## Acceptance

- Coverage reports reachability only and enforces a project-owned floor against a reviewed command.
- Fuzz evidence states the dated toolchain, target/seed identity and fixed run/time/length bounds;
  mutation evidence states selected operators/targets and caught/survived outcomes.
- Runtime never installs tools or fetches crates; scheduled/trusted-host needs are separate from PR
  evidence and every command has a finite outer deadline.
- Adapter/native outcomes agree on controlled fixtures, including each required failing sentinel.
- Full AWQ gates, hosted exact-head/main CI, signed/DCO publication, a public release and
  credential-free tagged installer/wheel/native-contract smoke pass.
